Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · Applies to the Dash software and the Dash Connector for QuickBooks Online

Who we are

DashRite Advisory LLC ("DashRite", "we") is a Florida limited liability company based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. Contact: dashriteadvisory@gmail.com.

What the Dash Connector does

When you connect QuickBooks Online, Dash reads your chart of accounts, customers, invoices, purchases and Profit & Loss report so you can compare them with your records in Dash. The connector is read-only: it does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.

What we collect

  • QuickBooks data you choose to sync. It is held only in your browser's memory for the session and is not written into Dash's books or stored on our servers.
  • A connection token. The access credential Intuit issues is encrypted on our server. Your browser keeps only the encrypted copy, which cannot be used without our server key.
  • Your company ID (realm ID), so the connector knows which QuickBooks company to read.

We do not collect payment card numbers, passwords or bank credentials.

How we use it

Only to show you your own QuickBooks data inside Dash and compare it with your Dash records. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is processed

The connector runs on Vercel (United States). Data passes from Intuit to your browser through that service over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Retention and your choices

Synced data is discarded when you close the session. You can disconnect at any time from Dash (Connectors → QuickBooks → Disconnect) or from your Intuit account's connected apps; either removes our access.

Security

Credentials are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, keys are kept server-side only, and all traffic uses HTTPS.

Children

Dash is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. Material changes will be noted in Dash.
